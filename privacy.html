Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

The short version

Hevyer keeps everything you log on your iPhone. There is no account, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking. Nothing you enter is collected by the developer or sent to anyone.

Who we are

Hevyer is a workout tracking app for iPhone, developed by Vebjørn Dahl in Norway (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information in the Hevyer app and on this website.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. Hevyer has no user accounts, no servers that receive your data, and no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter stays in the app on your iPhone, including:

  • Workouts, sets, routines, folders and programs
  • Goals, personal records and statistics
  • Body weight, body fat and body measurements
  • Custom exercises and their pictures
  • Your settings and preferences

We cannot see this information and have no copy of it.

Device permissions

Camera and photo library

Used only when you choose to add a picture to a custom exercise. The picture is stored on your device.

Notifications

Used to tell you when your rest timer is up. Notifications are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent through our servers.

Widgets and Live Activities

The Home Screen widgets and the Lock Screen / Dynamic Island activity show your own workout and goals, read from the app on your device.

You can change any of these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

When your data leaves your device

Only in two cases, both under your control:

  • Export Data. When you use Export Data in Hevyer's settings, a copy of your data is handed to the iOS share sheet and goes wherever you choose to send it.
  • Device backups. If you have turned on iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the app's data is included in those backups. Those are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Because all of your data is on your device, you are in full control of it. To erase everything, open Settings in Hevyer and choose Delete All Data, or delete the app from your iPhone.

Children

Hevyer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operation.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
